# Creative Brief

Revision: 1 · Status: draft

## Product and success
Product facts, primary audience/task, content hierarchy, intended outcome, scope and assumptions.

## Audience and emotion
Desired feeling and productive tension; how it supports the task.

## Territories and selection
For open direction: three distinct concepts, product fit/cost, selected direction and rejected tradeoffs. For fixed direction: describe it and relevant gaps.

## Creative DNA and Anti-Aesthetic
Observable typography, color, composition, shape/material, imagery rules; project-specific failure patterns.

## Narrative, motion and 3D strategy
What changes in understanding as the page progresses; motion purpose and temperament; rendering choice and rationale.

## Signature Moments
Meaning, trigger, state change, CTA, fallback and acceptance action for each moment.

## Evidence and uncertainties
Facts versus observed/inferred/chosen; reference mechanisms, evidence paths and unknowns. Never invent research observations.
