# Role contracts

Use these as logical duties, not instructions to launch agents. Delegate only when authorized and useful. Each request carries task scope, brief revision, product constraints, relevant artifacts, expected output and acceptance evidence. Each response carries decisions with reasons, artifact revision, evidence records, assumptions, unresolved issues and next owner.

| Duty | Input | Output | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Creative Director | Product/task/brand constraints and reference findings | Selected territory, DNA, Anti-Aesthetic, narrative and moments; Brief/Principles | Does not turn preferred tech into creative rationale |
| Reference Researcher | Product question and mechanisms to study | Reference cards, observed states, motion inventory, adaptable primitives | Observed behavior separate from inferred implementation; no promise of complete timeline recovery |
| Experience Designer | Brief and content/core task | Experience Map, states, action paths and equivalent fallbacks | Owns comprehension and flow rather than shader details |
| Motion Director | Map, direction, constraints | Timing tokens, choreography, property owners, reverse/reduced-motion behavior | Easing names are choices unless measured; no generic fade everywhere |
| Technical Director | Desired behaviors, actual repo and device targets | Implementation choices, asset strategy, budget and measurement plan | Sole budget owner; preserve working dependencies unless change is justified |
| Visual Critic | Brief, actual screenshots/motion, test environment and engineering results | Prioritized finding/interpretation/decision, visual score and limits | Does not rewrite product intent or infer motion from stills |

Reference card fields: id, URL or local path, observation date, viewport, action, observed behavior, evidence path, evidence_method, coverage, unknowns, inferred implementation, confidence, reusable mechanism and original reinterpretation. Missing evidence must be visible.

Each Signature Moment: product meaning, trigger, start/end states, readable content/CTA, timing or progress map, implementation candidate, owner, reverse path, keyboard/touch and reduced-motion equivalents, acceptance actions. Technical records whether the candidate is DOM/SVG/Canvas/3D and why.

Conflict priority: user/project requirements, selected direction, role assignment, optional tool preferences. Technical facts do not change because of taste. On conflict, modify implementation or record a scoped design exception. Unlicensed third-party skill text is not copied into this package.
